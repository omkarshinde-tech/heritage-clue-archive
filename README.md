# Heritage Clue Archive

Project Title: PhantomVault AI — An AI-Powered Haunted Heritage Detective

Problem Statement:

Historical recordings, forgotten family videos, old folk songs, and local oral histories contain valuable cultural memories. However, many recordings remain difficult to understand, search, and organize because they lack transcripts, subtitles, contextual descriptions, and structured metadata. Background noise, unclear speech, and missing historical information make these digital archives even harder to explore.

PhantomVault AI is an open-source, Halloween-themed digital heritage investigation platform that transforms old audio and video recordings into interactive mystery archives. Using speech recognition and the Gemma 4 AI model, the system generates transcripts, summarizes stories, identifies important keywords, builds event timelines, and answers questions about the content of recordings.

Its unique feature is a Mystery Clue Explorer, which presents important moments, unusual objects, recurring names, and historical references as clues that users can investigate. Users can search across recordings, explore timestamped evidence, correct metadata, and preserve the stories behind their collections.

The platform combines cultural preservation with an engaging Halloween mystery experience without claiming that AI can detect actual ghosts or supernatural activity.

Objective: To make forgotten cultural recordings searchable, understandable, and accessible through an open-source AI-powered investigation platform.          build a website on this and gemma 4 as backend
